Facade capture: click a wall (the face outlines; ◀ ▶ in the inspector cycles faces) → ⌘V a photo of it over the wall (or a queued image: Facade capture from this image) → drag the 4 corners → box sign / storefront / wall bands → it shows in the map live → Accept.
Logo / sign graphic: drag a picture from Google Images (e.g. "FamilyMart logo", a transparent PNG) onto the wall → it opens as logo (whole image into a box, transparent parts stay see-through) → move / resize the box on the face → Accept.
